### Privacy Policy for the Word Harvest App («Урожай слов»)

**Last updated:** November 1, 2026

**Word Harvest** (Russian name: **«Урожай слов»**; the “App”) is an offline word puzzle game for Android made with the Godot engine. The developer respects your privacy. This policy describes what data is processed in the App, where it is stored, and which third-party services are used.

#### 1. Developer

The App is distributed by **Vyacheslav Matusevich** (Android package: `ru.matusevichvv.sad_slov`). For privacy-related questions: **matu1@mail.ru**.

#### 2. What Data the Developer Collects

The developer **does not maintain user accounts**, the App has **no registration**, and the developer **does not request** your name, email address, phone number, or other personal data and **does not send** information about you to its own servers.

The game works offline; an internet connection is only needed to show ads, to run the analytics service and to receive settings (see below).

#### 3. Data Stored Only on Your Device

Game progress (for example, completed levels, in-game coins, hints, and settings) is saved **locally on your device** and is not intended to identify you personally. This data **is not synced** to the developer’s cloud. If you uninstall the App or reset your device data, it may be **permanently lost**. The developer is not responsible for such loss.

#### 4. Advertising (Third-Party Service)

The App contains ads and uses the **Yandex Mobile Ads** advertising platform (Yandex Advertising Network, Yandex LLC). The following are shown:

- **interstitial** ads — between levels;
- **rewarded video** ads — at your choice, for example to receive a hint or in-game coins;
- **banner** ads on some game screens.

To display and measure ads, the SDK connects to Yandex servers and may process, among other things: the **advertising identifier** of the device, technical device and OS parameters (model, OS version, language, network connection, IP address), and data about ad impressions and interactions. The scope and purposes of processing are defined by Yandex policies, not by the App developer. The App does not request access to location.

Current Yandex documents:

- [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/)
- [Yandex Mobile Ads SDK Terms of Use](https://yandex.ru/legal/mobileads_sdk_agreement/)

You can limit ad personalization by resetting or disabling the advertising ID in your Android device settings.

#### 5. Analytics and Remote Settings (Third-Party Services)

The App uses **AppMetrica** (Yandex analytics service) to collect anonymous usage statistics: for example, tutorial and level progress, technical events and errors, and words entered by the player that are not in the game’s dictionary. AppMetrica may automatically receive device and app data (device model, OS and App version, language, device and advertising identifiers, IP address). The **Varioqub** service (Yandex) may also be used to receive game settings from a server.

The scope and purposes of data processing by these services are defined by Yandex policies:

- [Yandex Privacy Policy](https://yandex.ru/legal/confidential/)
- [AppMetrica SDK Terms of Use](https://yandex.ru/legal/appmetrica_sdk_agreement/)

The developer uses these statistics only to improve the game.

#### 6. Purchases

The App has **no in-app purchases** for real money. In-game coins can only be earned in the game, including by watching ads.

#### 7. Network and Permissions

- The App uses **internet** and **network state** access to show ads, run analytics and receive settings.
- The App **does not request** access to the camera, microphone, contacts, calendar, user files, **location**, or other sensitive permissions.

#### 8. Children

The App is not intended to knowingly collect personal data from children, and the developer does not knowingly collect such data. Parents and guardians are advised to supervise children’s use of the App, including viewing ads. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the App, please write to **matu1@mail.ru**.

#### 9. Security

Game data remains on your device. The developer does not have remote access to it. Data sharing with third parties by the developer is limited to the cases described in the advertising and analytics sections (processing by Yandex services).

#### 10. Changes to This Policy

The developer may update this policy. The current version is published in this repository and/or in the app store together with updates. The date of the last change is shown at the top of this document.

#### 11. Contact

For privacy-related questions: **matu1@mail.ru**.

**Thank you for playing Word Harvest!**
